Help & support

We are here to help. Most questions are answered below, and you can always reach a real person.

Common questions

How do I get started with Lineup?

Go to lineup.mahoul.io, create an account, add your team and players, and build your first lineup. It works in any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer.

Is Lineup free?

Yes. The free plan covers a coach running one or two teams and is supported by ads. Paid plans add more teams and club, league and region features.

Who can create an account?

Accounts are for adults aged 18 and over, such as coaches, team parents and club administrators. Player profiles are created and managed by those adults.

How do I invite another coach or parent?

Open your team, choose to invite a member, and share the invite link that is created. They sign in with the link to join your team.

How do I share our schedule with parents?

Open your team's Calendar and choose Share. Pick the next 14, 30 or 90 days and send the link. Parents can view it without an account or add it to their own calendar app.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. You can export a full copy of a team's roster, games and stats from the app at any time. You can also delete a player or a team, or close your whole account. Deactivating your account keeps everything for a year so you can sign in and reactivate it; after that it is deleted. If you would rather have it deleted right away, choose permanent deletion, which has a 7-day grace period in case you change your mind.

Why do I see ads?

Ads keep the free plan free. Ads are provided by Google, and paid plans do not show ads.

I found a bug. What should I do?

Send it through Help & support in the app with the steps to reproduce it and, if possible, a screenshot. We read every report.
