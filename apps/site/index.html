Who we are

mahoul.io is a startup software company. We create simple applications that solve real-life problems for families, parents, coaches and athletes: juggling schedules, keeping things fair, and tracking what matters without a pile of spreadsheets and group texts. Every app starts with a real problem and aims to solve it in the simplest way possible.

🎯

Simple on purpose

Each app does one job well. No clutter, no training needed, and it works on any phone.

🤝

Built with real users

Features come from the people who use our apps every week, through Help & support inside each app.

🔒

Respectful of your data

Lineup

For youth soccer coaches and clubs · lineup.mahoul.io

Lineup helps coaches plan game day in minutes. Drag players onto the pitch to set any formation, and Lineup tracks who has played and who is owed time, so playing time stays fair across every game. Scores, minutes and stats roll up into season standings, and coaches can share a team calendar with parents.
